MYY LITTLE UNIVERSE — a free romantic photo museum

PREVIEW
- Open index.html in a browser.
- Keep the assets folder beside index.html. Do not rename or move the image files.
- The childhood photo of your girlfriend is the main hero image.
- Her photos are in “The Gallery of You”; your five photos are in “A little piece of me”.
- Tap any gallery photo to enlarge it. The final exhibit has the special question and interactive responses.

PUBLISH FOR FREE WITH GITHUB PAGES
1. Sign in to GitHub and create a new public repository (for example, my-little-universe).
2. Upload index.html, README.txt, and the entire assets folder.
3. Open the repository's Settings → Pages.
4. Under Build and deployment, select “Deploy from a branch”.
5. Select branch “main” and folder “/(root)”, then Save.
6. GitHub will publish a free website address for you. It may take a few minutes to appear.

IMPORTANT
This is a static website; there is no paid hosting or database needed. The YES / hug buttons show a response on the page but do not send you a notification or save her answer anywhere.
